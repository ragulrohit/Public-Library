About Us

More than books — a home for curious minds

Since our founding, Stackly Public Library has been a beacon of learning, culture and community connection.

Our Mission

Empowering everyone through access to knowledge

Stackly Public Library exists to make knowledge accessible, inclusive and joyful. We bring people and ideas together by curating diverse collections, nurturing literacy at every age and creating welcoming spaces where the whole community can learn, create and connect.

From toddlers discovering their first picture book to researchers uncovering new insights, we serve every stage of the lifelong learning journey.

Our Journey

Library history & achievements

1998

Stackly opens its doors with a modest collection of 4,000 books and a big dream.

2009

Expanded to a new building with dedicated children's and study wings.

2017

Launched the digital library, bringing e-books and databases online.

2024

Recognised as a "Community Library of the Year" for our reading outreach programmes.
